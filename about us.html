About Us

Changing lives through compassion, inclusion and opportunity.

Who We Are

Heart of Harmony Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to improving the lives of disabled children and orphans through education, healthcare, shelter, nutrition and emotional support.

We believe that every child deserves love, dignity, equal opportunities and the chance to achieve their dreams regardless of their background or disability.

Our Mission

To provide care, education and empowerment that enables disabled children and orphans to live healthy, confident and independent lives.

Our Vision

A society where every child is valued, protected and given equal opportunities to reach their full potential.

Our Core Values

Love

We serve every child with compassion and kindness.

Respect

We value every child regardless of ability.

Inclusion

Everyone deserves equal opportunities.

Integrity

We remain honest, transparent and accountable.
